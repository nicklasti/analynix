About
Nixreenr grades U.S.-listed stocks against their industry peers using public market data.

Built by Nick Lastihenos.
How grades work
Each stock is ranked against its industry on eleven metrics: revenue, revenue growth, EBITDA growth, forward EPS growth, earnings, profit margin, P/E, forward P/E, EPS, P/S and P/B. Industries with too few stocks are compared with their sector instead.

Each ranking becomes a letter grade on a bell curve: roughly the top 3% get an A+, the middle 14% a C and the bottom 3% an F-. Missing data is skipped.

The Stock Rating combines all the metric grades with an AI analyst's grade and ranks the result against every stock. Top Rated means B or better; Low Rated means D or worse.
Data
Fundamentals and headlines come from Finviz, prices from Yahoo Finance, and the AI summaries from OpenAI. Prices refresh live when you open a stock. Headlines update every weeknight; fundamentals, grades and AI summaries every Saturday.
